거대한 사건의 지평선이 화면 오른쪽 2/3을 채우고, 그 경계 **안쪽에 나선 은하 무리가 갇힌 듯** 떠 있는 구도.

```
Photorealistic cinematic deep space scene. A colossal black hole event horizon rendered as a vast perfectly dark sphere occupying the right two-thirds of the frame, its edge rimmed by a thin luminous accretion glow transitioning from cyan-teal to warm orange. Inside the horizon boundary, a small cluster of spiral galaxies floats, clearly enclosed and dwarfed by the curved dark edge, their light appearing faintly trapped. Deep navy-black background with sparse distant stars. Cold cyan rim light against the absolute void, dramatic scale contrast, ominous and quiet atmosphere. Lower third of the frame intentionally darker and uncluttered. No text, no people, no cartoon or illustrated elements.
```

```

An immense black hole horizon curves across the frame as a smooth void, with gravitational lensing visibly stretching and arcing the light of galaxies caught inside its boundary into thin luminous crescents. Teal-cyan lensed light streaks bend around the dark curvature, with orange-amber highlights at the brightest arc points. Ultra-deep navy background, astrophotography grade detail, sense of enormous enclosed scale. Composition weighted to upper two-thirds, lower band left dark and clean. No text, no human figures, no illustration style.
```

On the left pan rests a small luminous cluster of spiral galaxies glowing cyan and warm white; on the right pan sits a pure black sphere with a thin orange accretion rim. The beam is exactly horizontal, emphasizing an impossible perfect match. Deep navy-black void background, dramatic side lighting, polished dark metal with subtle cyan reflections, tense and uncanny mood. Lower third kept dark and empty. No text, no people, no cartoon elements.

At the innermost center sits a single small spiral galaxy, tiny and warm-orange, appearing utterly enclosed by layer after layer of darkness. Deep navy-black field, strong depth perspective, recursive infinite feeling, quiet dread. Bottom portion of frame darker for overlay. No text, no figures, no illustrated or cartoon rendering.

The right half mirrors it exactly but reversed: a brilliant burst pouring luminous matter and warm orange-white light outward from a bright core. A sharp vertical seam divides the two halves, emphasizing perfect time-reversed symmetry. Deep navy-black background, high dynamic contrast between devouring darkness and erupting light, dramatic and uncanny. Lower band left dark and uncluttered. No text, no people, no cartoon style.
