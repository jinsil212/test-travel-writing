Galaxies near the center are sharp and luminous with warm orange cores and cyan-teal nebula filaments. Toward the right edge, the galaxies progressively fade, lose detail, and dissolve into pure darkness in a soft vanishing gradient, as if being erased from existence. Deep navy-black background. Melancholic, quiet, vast. Lower third intentionally darker and uncluttered. No text, no people, no cartoon or illustrated elements.
