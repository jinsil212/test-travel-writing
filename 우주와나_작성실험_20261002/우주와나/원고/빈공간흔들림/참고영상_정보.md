## 설명란 (원문)

> What is space? Is it an empty stage, a dynamical geometry or something that emerges from quantum entanglement? Space remains one of the deepest mysteries in physics. It challenged Newton, Leibniz, and Einstein, and even today we do not fully understand its fundamental nature. In this video, we follow the idea of space from classical physics and general relativity to the quantum vacuum, quantum gravity, holography, AdS/CFT, and emergent spacetime.
